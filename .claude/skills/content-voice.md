# Voice & Stance Guidelines: Quiet Confidence

When writing content about the business, origin story, products, or achievements, use a tone of **quiet confidence and understated achievement**.

## Core Principles
*   **Factual & Specific:** Be clear about what was built, shipped, and learned. Do not hide real results.
*   **No Hype or Bragging:** Do not use language that sounds like performance or self-praise. Avoid: "incredible," "amazing," "industry-leading," "groundbreaking," etc.
*   **Concrete over Evaluative:** Prefer plain verbs and numbers over adjectives. ("We reduced latency from 320ms to 45ms" instead of "We built an incredibly fast system.")
*   **Let Achievements Speak:** State them matter-of-factly. Do not add layers like "which is rare" or "few people can do this."
*   **Direct & Unembellished:** When describing challenges, be direct. No heroic framing. No "against all odds" narratives.
*   **Underpromise & Overdeliver:** Describe current capabilities conservatively. Avoid hype about the future.
*   **No Performative Humility:** Avoid self-aggrandizing constructions like "I'm proud to say" or "I'm humbled to announce."
*   **Low Emotional Temperature:** Calm, grounded, and secure.

## Applying to Origin Stories
*   Focus on decisions, constraints, and trade-offs, not heroics.
*   Mention failures and pivots as normal parts of building, not as dramatic turning points.
*   Keep the narrative tight and honest. No motivational clichés.
