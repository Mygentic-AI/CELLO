CELLO's target user is NOT "developers." It is anyone who uses AI agents (Claude Code, Hermes, OpenClaw, Codex, Claude Co-work, etc.) and needs to collaborate with a colleague who also uses AI agents.

**Why:** The people using these tools today are not just engineers. They are GTM professionals, consultants, analysts, operators — anyone using AI agents to do their work. When they need to hand off work or collaborate with a colleague, they fall back to copy-pasting into email, Slack, shared docs, or other ad hoc methods. These are all substitutes for true agent-to-agent communication.

**The real pain:** Not "how do I build a secure identity layer" — but "I'm using my AI agent to do work and I need to share it / collaborate with another person using their AI agent, and right now that's clunky and lossy."

**CELLO removes that friction.** It is the communication layer between people who work *through* AI agents, not just between developers *building* AI systems.

**How to apply:** Positioning, landing page copy, content templates, one-sentence pitch, and ICP definition in the master playbook should all reflect this. The audience is "AI agent users who collaborate" — not "developers." Lead with the collaboration friction problem, not the cryptographic mechanism.
