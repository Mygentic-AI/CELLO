CELLO's target user is NOT "developers." It is anyone using AI agents (Claude Code, Hermes, OpenClaw, Codex, etc.) who needs to collaborate with a colleague also using AI agents.

The audience includes GTM professionals, consultants, analysts, operators — people who use AI agents to do their work but are not necessarily engineers.

The pain: when they need to collaborate or hand off work, they fall back to copy-pasting into email, Slack, docs — ad hoc substitutes for real agent-to-agent communication.

CELLO removes that friction. It is the communication layer between people who work *through* AI agents.

**Why:** Andre corrected this explicitly (2026-07-17). "Developer" framing is a wedge but not the full picture and leads to narrow positioning.

**How to apply:** All positioning, pitch copy, content templates, and ICP language should lead with "AI agent users who collaborate" — not "developers building AI systems."
